    <h1 id="Location" class="mb-4 text-center">Conference Location</h1>

    <p>ICSOB 2026 will be held at the <b>Lindholmen Conference Centre</b>, Lindholmspiren 3–5, 402 78 Gothenburg, Sweden, October 26–28, 2026. The venue sits inside Lindholmen Science Park, on the north waterfront of the Göta Älv river, a few minutes from central Gothenburg.</p>

    <p>Gothenburg (Göteborg) is Sweden's second-largest city, located on the west coast. It is easily accessible via Gothenburg Landvetter Airport (GOT), with regular flight connections from major European cities.</p>

    <h2 class="mb-3">How to Reach Lindholmen Conference Centre</h2>

    <p>The notes below are aimed especially at travellers coming from outside Sweden who are not familiar with local transport.</p>

    <h3 class="h3 mt-4">By air</h3>
    <p><b>Gothenburg Landvetter Airport (GOT)</b> is the closest airport, about 25 km east of the city centre, with connections to many major European hubs.</p>
    <ul>
        <li><b><a href="https://www.flygbussarna.se/en" target="_blank">Flygbussarna</a></b> airport coach runs directly to Nils Ericson Terminalen, next to Gothenburg Central Station, in about 35 minutes, with 2–3 departures per hour. Tickets are sold online or via app — the coaches do not accept cash.</li>
        <li><b>Taxi</b> takes about 25 minutes, traffic permitting.</li>
        <li>Budget coach alternatives such as <b><a href="https://www.flixbus.com" target="_blank">FlixBus</a></b> and <b><a href="https://www.vybuss.com/airport-bus/landvetter--gothenburg-airport/" target="_blank">Vy bus4you</a></b> also serve the airport, typically at a lower frequency.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>If you are travelling from outside Europe or cannot find a convenient direct flight to Gothenburg, <b>Copenhagen Airport (CPH)</b> in Denmark is a well-connected alternative hub, roughly 300 km south. From Copenhagen Airport, the <b><a href="https://www.oresundstag.se/en/travel-information/buy-ticket" target="_blank">Öresundståg</a></b> train runs directly to Gothenburg Central Station roughly once an hour, with a journey time of around 3.5 hours — no need to change stations in Copenhagen.</p>

    <h3 class="h3 mt-4">By train</h3>
    <p>Gothenburg Central Station has frequent long-distance connections within Sweden (including Stockholm) and to Copenhagen/Denmark via the Öresundståg service mentioned above. International visitors already elsewhere in Scandinavia may find the train a comfortable way to reach Gothenburg.</p>

    <h3 class="h3 mt-4">From Gothenburg Central Station to Lindholmen</h3>
    <p>Lindholmen is on the island of Hisingen, just across the river from the city centre, and is reachable in a few minutes by bus or ferry:</p>
    <ul>
        <li><b>Bus:</b> Line 16 is the most frequent service to the "Lindholmen" stop, running every 3–10 minutes; several other lines (23, 30, 31, 55, 99, 126, 165, 199, 402, 510) also stop there.</li>
        <li><b>Ferry:</b> The free <b>Älvsnabbare</b> passenger ferry crosses the river from Stenpiren to Lindholmen in a couple of minutes, departing every 10 minutes (approximately 07:00–18:00) — a scenic alternative to the bus.</li>
    </ul>
    <p>Public transport in the region is operated by <b><a href="https://www.vasttrafik.se/en/" target="_blank">Västtrafik</a></b>; tickets can be bought through the <a href="https://www.vasttrafik.se/en/Tickets/more-about-tickets/vasttrafik-to-go/" target="_blank">Västtrafik To Go app</a>, and the <a href="https://www.vasttrafik.se/en/" target="_blank">Västtrafik journey planner</a> can help with routing from anywhere in the region to Lindholmen.</p>
